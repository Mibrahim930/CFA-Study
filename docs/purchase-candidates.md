# Purchase candidates for coverage gaps (research only)

Researched 2026-10-10. Nothing was purchased, no accounts were created and no personal data was entered. Only candidates under US$20 per item are shortlisted.

## Headline findings

1. **Almost nothing cheap is usable by the ingest pipeline.** Every sub-$20 item found is a Kindle or print book, or a Udemy video course. All of these are DRM-locked, print-only or streaming-only. The only DRM-free sellers found were **Leanpub** (PDF/EPUB, DRM-free by design) and, unverified, a few small publishers. The one Leanpub title that is relevant, *Intuitive Statistics*, is a general statistics book and covers only part of Quant.
2. **The Amazon "CFA Level 1 2026/2027 study guide" market is dominated by low-credibility titles.** These have no named author, or an author with no verifiable credentials, and often carry 0 to 5 reviews. Examples are "High Finance Academy", "ThinkVest Labs", "Re-Wise Publishers", and several "1000+ practice question" guides. Treat all of them as unverified. Several likely draw heavily on, or paraphrase, the curriculum, which the brief says to avoid.
3. **Official and reputable titles are over budget.** The CFA Institute Investment Series is $65 to $76 per title (print). The 2026 and 2027 curriculum box sets cost $189 to $255. The 2027 official Prerequisite Reading volumes are $25. FinQuiz's formula sheet is $39. PrepNuggets' own site is $159 and its Udemy partnership has ended, so those Udemy courses are frozen at the 2023 curriculum.
4. **Realistic use:** purchases below are for the user's own study (reading and watching), not for loading into the corpus, except the Leanpub item. If the goal is more grounding text, the better route is more free or open sources (see Section 4).

## 4. DRM and pipeline usability

| Seller | DRM / file | Usable by the pipeline? |
|---|---|---|
| Amazon Kindle (all the Re-Wise, ThinkVest ebook, Coventry, Little Book, Shah etc.) | Kindle DRM; the "print replica" format is a page image | **No.** Do not circumvent. |
| Amazon print | Paper | No (scanning and OCR of copyrighted text is outside the brief) |
| Udemy | Streaming; offline viewing only inside the Udemy app; no notes or slides download observed for the Sharma course | No. Terms limit use to personal viewing; I did not read the full terms |
| Leanpub | DRM-free PDF/EPUB (verified from the page listing formats) | **Yes** for private grounding. Check each book's licence line: the usual Leanpub licence is personal use |
| Wiley / CFA Institute Investment Series ebooks | DRM (via retailers such as Perlego/VitalSource) | No, and over budget |
| Gumroad (unnamed seller) | PDF, DRM usually absent, but not verified | Possibly; low-reliability seller |
| Free alternatives (not purchases; not verified here) | OpenStax *Principles of Finance*, *Principles of Economics*, *Introductory Business Statistics*; CFA Institute Research Foundation PDFs on ML/big data and geopolitical themes | Worth a check against `docs/missing-sources.md`; these directly target quant-11, econ-05 and corpfin gaps and need no purchase |

Practical advice: if the aim is to ground generated content, spend on a few reputable DRM-free titles and open sources rather than on Kindle books. If the aim is to learn, the Udemy course plus the calculation workbook is the best value.
